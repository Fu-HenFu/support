## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Does FeedSmith require an active internet connection?
**No.** FeedSmith is built strictly for the off-grid. All NASEM mathematical algorithms, species databases, batch scaling, and PDF generations execute 100% locally on your device's internal processor. You can formulate rations in deep metal barns, remote valleys, or airplane mode with zero cell service.

### 2. Is FeedSmith Pro a monthly subscription?
**No.** FeedSmith operates on an honest, one-time lifetime license. There are no monthly fees, no yearly renewals, and no surprise charges. 

### 3. Does my Pro purchase support Family Sharing?
**Yes.** FeedSmith Pro is fully eligible for Apple Family Sharing. Once unlocked, up to 5 family members linked to your Apple Family group can restore and access all Pro features across their iPhones and iPads at no additional cost.

### 4. How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone or iPad?
1. Open FeedSmith.
2. Navigate to **Settings** (top right gear icon).
3. Under the *Support & Actions* section, tap **Restore Purchases**.
4. Your Apple ID entitlements will sync instantly from your local Apple credentials.

### 5. Why are my mixed ingredients not adding up to the target protein?
The classic Pearson Square balancer requires one high-protein feed (above your target CP) and one low-protein energy grain (below your target CP). If you select two low-protein feeds (e.g., Corn at 8.5% and Oats at 11.5%) to reach a 16.5% target, the app will safely alert you of an `Impossible Mix` to protect your livestock from nutritional deficiency.

## 🔒 Official Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2024  
**Developer:** FeedSmith Software

### 1. Zero Data Collection
FeedSmith does **NOT** collect, transmit, track, log, or monetize any personal information, telemetry, device identifiers, or analytics. 
* No user accounts, registration, or logins are required.
* No third-party SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising frameworks are embedded in the application.
* No outbound network requests are initiated by the application.

### 2. Local Data Retention
All formulations, custom mixing sheets, and user preferences are stored strictly on your device's local internal storage via Apple SwiftData. The developer cannot access, view, or recover your farm data under any circumstances. You may permanently delete all local data at any time via the in-app Settings menu.

### 3. Financial Transactions
In-App Purchases are processed solely and securely by Apple Inc. via StoreKit 2. We never see or store your credit card, billing address, or payment credentials.

### 4. Legal Compliance
Because zero data is harvested, FeedSmith is inherently and fully compliant with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## ⚠️ Veterinary & Nutritional Disclaimer

Feed formulations generated by FeedSmith are mathematical approximations derived from published scientific guidelines (including NASEM/NRC standards) and are intended solely for nutritional and educational guidance.

* FeedSmith does **NOT** provide certified veterinary prescriptions or clinical medical treatments.
* Individual livestock responses vary based on genetics, microclimates, feedstuff quality, and pathogen load.
* Always introduce new rations gradually over a **7 to 14 day transition window** to prevent acute ruminal acidosis, bloat, or gastrointestinal stasis.
* Consult a licensed Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) or certified animal nutritionist before executing significant dietary changes.
